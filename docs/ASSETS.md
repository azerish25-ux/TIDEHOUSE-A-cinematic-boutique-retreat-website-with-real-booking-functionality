## Coherent final-series art direction

The original image-generation tool was not available in this build environment. Do not describe these photos as generated or as a coherent shoot of the five designed cabins. A final replacement series should use one master property reference and consistent architecture for every view.

Master direction: a modest five-cabin North Atlantic retreat; weathered cedar and dark bronze roof details; dune grasses, granite and a quiet shoreline; contemporary but not glossy; realistic construction joints, water marks and material variation. Natural overcast coastal light or plausible low-angle morning sun, never impossible multiple light sources. No oversaturated skies, exaggerated lens glow, plastic linen, duplicated furniture or geometry that changes between views.

Required shots: one wide property / shoreline hero; an exterior and a faithful interior for each of the five cabins; two detail images of linen / joinery; a breakfast scene; the actual proposed sauna; and three editorial landscape images. Keep the Salt, Dune, Drift, Cove and Pine layouts aligned with their concept plans, guest capacities and access claims. Verify all images at full resolution, including reflections, furniture legs, doorways, stairs, roof drainage and coastline continuity.
